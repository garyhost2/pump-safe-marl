# DHW v2 — RL v3: setpoint residual policies, behaviour cloning, and the headroom study

Branch `dhw-v2-rl`. Plant and filter: dhw-v2 v2.1 frozen defaults (oracle filter, α = 0.05).
Swedish site-year packages from the matrix track's converter, with each package's borehole.
Every number below is read from `experiments/dhw_v2_rl/results_v3/` (generated by `make_report_v3.py`
from `experiments/dhw_v2_rl/runs_v3/`).

**Bottom line.**
- **The headroom exists.** In Karlstad 2025 the causal MPC that uses the building's thermal mass
  (MPC-band, band [20, 23] °C) beats Price-aware by 4 215 SEK on the
  objective. Most of that comes from running the houses nearer 20 °C. At the same or better comfort
  (band [21, 22]) MPC-band still costs 1 243 SEK less than Price-aware, with
  fewer K h below 21 °C.
- **RL v3 beats every rule.** The full RL v3 method (residual setpoints + behaviour cloning on MPC
  trajectories + PPO, variant A) beats the best rule, the tuned price-threshold pre-heat rule, on every
  seed in Karlstad 2025 (−493 [-781, -204] (5/5) SEK). It beats Price-aware by
  2 828 SEK.
- **RL v3 still loses to MPC-band.** It does not reach the causal MPC-band
  (+1 387 [1 098, 1 676] (0/5) SEK, 0/5 seeds better) or the perfect-foresight oracle.
- **PPO fine-tuning made the cloned policy worse.** Behaviour cloning alone (variant C) is better than
  BC + PPO: −1 048 [-1 085, -1 010] (3/3) vs the best rule, +832 [795, 870] (0/3) vs MPC-band.
- **Without cloning, PPO adds nothing.** Residual PPO without BC (B) stays at the baseline
  (+190 [-536, 917] (1/3)). Non-residual setpoint PPO (D) is worse than the baseline
  (+2 836 [-341, 6 013] (0/3)).

## 1. Headroom (filtered, held-out sets) — first, as requested

**Objective.** At comfort band [lo, hi] the objective is:
cost + 20 SEK per building-hour with the DHW top node below 45 °C + 5 SEK per K h of indoor
temperature below lo + 0.1 SEK per compressor start + 50 SEK per missed disinfection week.
For Texas the penalties are converted at 10.28 SEK/USD. Hours ABOVE the band are reported in the CSV
but not priced. In the Swedish summer every controller floats above 23 °C on solar and internal gains
with the heating off, which no controller can change. The amount depends on the site: per-controller
means are 25 229–28 585 K h (Karlstad 2024), 26 575–27 730 K h (Karlstad 2025) and
36 382–37 344 K h (Luleå 2025, 34–37 % more than Karlstad 2025 for the same controller); Texas has 0.

**Controllers.**
- **MPC-band** (`stems/dhw_v2_rl/mpc_v3.py`): an LP over 24 h for all houses every hour. It models
  the DHW store, the plant's own 5R1C building as an exact affine map (checked to 1e-14 against
  `_solve`), compressor time sharing, DHW electricity at the current-hour COP, and space-heating (SH)
  electricity. The SH electricity uses the slope of the map's affine upper bound at the
  heating-curve supply temperature. In this plant the supply temperature follows the outdoor
  temperature, not the SH power, so no power-dependent COP is needed.
  - Weather over the horizon = the realised data (a perfect weather forecast, ASSUMPTION).
  - Prices: the next 24 h. Draws: causal forecast + margin.
  - The DHW reserve ρ = 0.9 comes from the fair-round tuning.
- **PF oracle**: the same LP, 72-h horizon, realised draws and prices (not a plant bound).
- **Pre-heat rule**: Price-aware DHW; indoor setpoint 23 °C in the 4 cheapest hours of the next 24,
  20 °C in the 8 most expensive, 21 °C otherwise. These parameters are the best of 8 candidates on
  Karlstad 2024 (objective 62 015 vs Price-aware 63 814).
- **Texas**: demand-mode buildings. Heat above the demand is lost, so the "band" there can only defer
  heat down to 20 °C (debt model); there is no pre-heating.

## 2. RL v3

**Design** (`stems/dhw_v2_rl/rl_v3.py`, `experiments/dhw_v2_rl/train_v3.py`).

- **Action, per house per hour:** δ ∈ [−1, 1]², executed by a thermostat layer. No raw power fractions.
  - δ_d shifts Price-aware's DHW hysteresis thresholds by 8δ_d K. The stop threshold is capped at
    54 °C and the top-node trigger stays at 47 °C. The compressor runs whole hours while "on".
  - δ_s shifts the pre-heat rule's indoor setpoint by 1.5δ_s K, clipped to [20, 23] °C.
  - Disinfection is Price-aware's request plus the filter's robust trigger.
- **Residual check:** the mean head is zero-initialised, so the untrained policy is exactly the
  pre-heat rule. Verified: Karlstad 2025 objective 56 557 SEK for the zero-init
  policy (variant Z) vs 56 557 for the rule; cost 53501.2 vs 53501.2.
  A 300-h check matched to every digit.
- **Observation (101 values):**
  - 24-h price, normalised by the Karlstad 2022–2024 mean;
  - 24-h outdoor temperature (realised);
  - 24-h causal draw profile and the last 3 h of draws;
  - 10 tank nodes;
  - indoor and building-mass temperature;
  - brine temperatures;
  - q_max_s and the need to reach the setpoint;
  - weekly disinfection state;
  - baseline setpoint, baseline DHW state and a cheap-hour flag.
- **Reward:** −(cost + 20 SEK per building-hour with the DHW top node below 45 °C + 5 SEK/K h below
  20 °C + 50 SEK per missed week + 0.1 SEK/start + the correction penalty k = 1).
  - Above-23 °C hours are not priced: they are uncontrollable in summer.
  - Normalisation is separate: divide by the training mean price, then PPO's running return std.
- **Behaviour cloning:** MPC-band [20,23] (causal) was run on Karlstad 2022, 2023 and 2024, giving
  210 432 house-hours.
  - The SH target is δ_s* = (planned indoor temperature − baseline setpoint)/1.5. The fit reached
    MSE 0.0089 against a target sd of 0.67.
  - The DHW target is δ_d* = 0, i.e. DHW kept at the baseline. The MPC's charge decision has no unique
    inverse in setpoint space; this is declared.
- **PPO:**
  - γ 0.99, λ 0.95, clip 0.2, 10 epochs, minibatch 512, rollout 546 h × 8 houses, lr 3e-4, 128-128 network.
  - Gaussian exploration with std initialised at 0.3.
  - Variant A adds a BC regulariser, weight 1 decaying to 0 over the first 30 % of updates.
- **Training:** random 13-week windows from Karlstad 2022–2024, 450 000 h × 8 houses per run, about
  3 h per run. 5 seeds for A, 3 each for B, C and D.
- **Evaluation:** Karlstad 2024 (in-sample) is used for every choice. The tests are Karlstad 2025
  and Luleå 2025 (out-of-distribution, Karlstad policies). RL v3 was not run on Texas: there the
  buildings are demand-mode, with no thermal mass to act on.

**Ablation reading.**
- **A vs C:** PPO fine-tuning moved the cloned policy away from the MPC behaviour and lost about
  555 SEK (Karlstad 2025).
- **B:** without the MPC teacher, residual PPO found essentially nothing beyond the baseline in
  450k steps.
- **D:** without the residual, setpoint PPO is worse than the baseline.
- So the gain over the rules comes from imitation of MPC-band, not from reinforcement learning.

**Did the headroom make a win possible?**
- **Over the rules, yes:** every A and C seed is better than the tuned pre-heat rule in Karlstad 2025
  and 2024. In Luleå 2025, 3/3 C seeds and 4/5 A seeds are better.
- **Over MPC-band, no:** no RL variant reaches it. The closest is C, which is
  832 SEK worse in Karlstad 2025 and
  985 SEK worse in Luleå 2025.

## 3. Results against us

1. **RL v3 still loses to MPC-band** (causal) in every evaluation: 0/5 A seeds and 0/3 seeds of
   B, C and D are better, on all three Swedish sets. It also loses to the PF oracle everywhere.
2. **The best RL result is BC only;** PPO fine-tuning degraded it. "RL made to work" here means
   "imitation of an MPC made to work". The reinforcement-learning step itself did not help in this
   budget.
3. **RL without the MPC teacher does not beat the tuned rule:** B is at the baseline and D is worse.
4. **Most of the headroom is comfort, not price shifting.** At equal comfort ([21,22]) the
   thermal-mass shift is worth about 2.2 % causal and
   3.7 % PF in Karlstad 2025. The [20,23] numbers include running
   the houses colder.
5. **Starts:** MPC-band starts the compressor about 3.4× as often as Price-aware
   (13 213 vs 3 871). RL A starts 5 073. The 0.1 SEK start
   price does not stop this.
6. **The filter's intra-hour certificate is not exact on Swedish 5-min draws.** Unexplained
   violations are non-zero for Null (55/74) and Thermia (5/6), and 1–2 h for MPC
   (`violations_unexplained` in the CSV).
7. **Simplified MPC:** perfect 24/72-h weather, the current-hour brine and COP held over the horizon,
   and SH electricity priced by the map's affine upper bound. The perfect-draw oracle is a reference,
   not a bound.
8. **Luleå houses H01 and H03 exceed the RXT 12's 12 kW at the design temperature,** with no auxiliary
   heater. Their K h below 20 °C enter every controller's objective. The lower-band violations are
   large for every controller there.

## Budget and deviations (declared)

- **Training:** 450k steps per PPO run, 2.8–3.3 h each; 11 PPO runs in parallel, 17–20 processes in
  total.
- **Seeds:** 5 for A (the declared full method) and 3 each for B, C and D. A was fixed as the final
  variant in advance, not selected afterwards; on 2024 the best variant was C.
- **No IQL/AWAC.**
- **Hyperparameters:** PPO settings come from the fair round, not re-tuned for v3; the BC regulariser
  schedule was not tuned. The DHW BC target is 0.
- **RL scope:** no RL v3 on Texas, and no Luleå training.
- **Validation:** Karlstad 2024 is used for every choice, but it is inside the RL training years, so
  it is in-sample for RL. For the pre-heat rule tuning it is a genuine validation, since the rule is
  not trained.
